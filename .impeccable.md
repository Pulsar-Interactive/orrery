## Design Context

### Users
TouchDesigner artists, and the clients and venue staff they hand the phone to, run a TouchDesigner project from a phone. The artist sets it up; the person holding the phone may not be technical and doesn't know how the patches are built. They use it in the studio or at rehearsal (next to the Mac, with time to look) and live in a dark venue (one hand, quick glances, any mistake shows on the big screen). Their job: find the right COMP and nudge a few controls without breaking the show. Nobody builds patches on the phone.

### Brand Personality
Pulsar Interactive brand v1.0 (see the pulsar-brand skill): Space Grotesk for display and labels, Inter for body, cyan brand color, the glitch used only as a tiny accent. Tone: a calm, precise instrument, like good hardware. Quiet and steady with no surprises. The art lives on the big screen, not on the phone.

### Aesthetic Direction
Restrained and utilitarian. It is a brand-mandated font pair, so the generic reflex-font rules don't apply. Dark theme only, for dark venues. Status (connected, on air, recording) must be readable at a glance in any light. Anti-references: busy dashboards, neon-on-dark "VJ app" clichés, anything that looks fragile or experimental to a client.

### Design Principles
1. What's on air, and what will change if I tap: always obvious.
2. Recover from mistakes in one tap (undo, confirmation for show-breaking actions).
3. Big targets, one-handed reach, readable in the dark.
4. Use plain words, never TD internals (op names, par names, paths).
5. Keep it quiet: one accent, used for meaning.
